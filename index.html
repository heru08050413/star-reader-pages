👨‍👩‍👧 Parent & Teacher Guide

Tailored specifically for an 8-year-old girl learning English as a second language.

🌟 The 15-Minute Daily ESL Routine

  1. Step 1: Listen First (3 mins): Click "Read to Me" on Turtle (0.8x) speed. Let her listen and look at the highlighted words.
  2. Step 2: Read Together (5 mins): Read the story out loud together. If she stumbles on a word, she can click it to hear the pronunciation!
  3. Step 3: Check Vocabulary (2 mins): Tap the highlighted pink words with ✨ to learn what they mean. Click "Add to My Word Bank".
  4. Step 4: Quiz & Talk Time (5 mins): Let her answer the 3 quiz questions and answer the daily speaking challenge prompt out loud.

📱 How to Open on iPad or Tablet

  1. Double-click start_for_ipad.bat on this computer.
  2. Connect your iPad to the same home Wi-Fi.
  3. Open Safari on iPad and enter: http://192.168.3.24:8000
  4. Tap Safari's Share button (arrow pointing up) ➡️ "Add to Home Screen" to install it full-screen like a real App!
